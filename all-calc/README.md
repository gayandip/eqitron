# All in one investment calculator

#### This calculator has:

- Discounted cash flow (DCF), how much all the future cashflows worth today
- Calculate current valuation based on future assumed profit or sales and assumed multiples
- calculate CAGR
- calculate eps based on current quarter
- requird earnings growth for desired returns, based on assumed exit multiples and current price
- price valuation based on required returns, exit multiple and earnings growth
- Maximum loss protection capping, fist buying quantity in percentage then the maximum loss you are comfortable with. This means upto that percentage of drop in price which you are getting your maximum loss will be as you wanted. The buying pattern should be like you are doubling your position when you hit your maximum capicity of loss. This can also be extended with some minimum manual efforts.
<hr>
Disclaimer : all calculations are assumption based and linear calculation but market is non-linear, so never be too much optimistic, and always calculate your margin of safety. These are just helping tools, don't assure accuracy.
